### 📖 About me

I'm a doctoral researcher at **Tecnológico de Monterrey** working on semantic segmentation of volumetric medical images. My thesis explores **Explainable AI and Federated Learning in Medicine**, with applications to MRI and ureteroscopy.

I build end-to-end PyTorch pipelines, from DICOM preprocessing to training and longitudinal inference, and fine-tune vision transformer foundation models with LoRA on multicenter data. I trained as a biomedical engineer, so I've also built the sensors that collect EEG, EMG and ECG data.
